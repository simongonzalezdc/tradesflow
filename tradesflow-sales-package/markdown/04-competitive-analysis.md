## Market Landscape

The field service management software market is crowded, but most solutions are either:

1. **Too generic** — Built for any service business, not trade-specific
2. **Too complex** — Enterprise features that solo techs don't need
3. **Too expensive** — Per-user pricing that penalizes growth

TradesFlow occupies a unique position: **purpose-built for trades, simple enough for solo operators, powerful enough for teams.**

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Best Fit for TradesFlow

**Company Size**:
- Solo technicians to 50+ employee companies
- Especially strong for 1-20 technician operations

**Trade Types**:
- HVAC contractors
- Plumbing companies
- Electrical contractors
- Roofing businesses
- Appliance repair services

**Business Stage**:
- Growing businesses ready to professionalize
- Companies outgrowing spreadsheets and paper
- Teams frustrated with per-user pricing

**Characteristics**:
- Value simplicity over feature bloat
- Want equipment tracking and history
- Prefer transparent, predictable pricing
- Need mobile access for field staff

### Less Ideal Fit

- Very large enterprises (500+ technicians) — consider ServiceTitan
- Businesses needing complex inventory management — we're building this
- Companies requiring extensive third-party integrations today — we're expanding
