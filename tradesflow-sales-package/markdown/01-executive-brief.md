## The Three Problems That Cost You Money

### 1. Lost Customer Information
Spreadsheets, sticky notes, and memory gaps mean lost jobs and frustrated customers. **Every time you can't instantly recall a customer's history, you lose credibility.**

### 2. Scheduling Chaos
Double-bookings, missed appointments, and endless phone tag waste hours every week. **Poor scheduling can cost a trade business $20,000+ annually in lost productivity.**

### 3. Invoice & Follow-Up Failures
Forgetting to send invoices or follow up on quotes means money left on the table. **40% of service quotes never convert due to lack of follow-up.**

## Equipment Passport™: Our Signature Differentiator *(In Development — Expected Q2 2026)*

**What if you could remember every detail about every customer's equipment?**

The Equipment Passport is a complete digital record of every HVAC unit, water heater, electrical panel, and appliance at your customer's property:

- **Full specifications**: Brand, model, serial number, installation date
- **Warranty tracking**: Never miss an expiration—proactively reach out
- **Photo documentation**: Nameplates, installations, conditions
- **Complete service history**: Every repair, maintenance visit, and inspection logged

**Result**: When a customer calls, you have the complete story in seconds. You look like a genius.
