## What is TradesFlow?

TradesFlow is an app that helps trade businesses run their day-to-day operations from one place. Think of it as a digital headquarters for HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, roofers, and appliance repair shops — a single tool that replaces the spreadsheets, sticky notes, phone calls, and paper files most trade businesses rely on today.

It works for everyone from a single person running their own business to companies with 20 or more employees. It's especially well-suited for small-to-medium businesses with 1 to 10 team members.

## The problems it solves

Running a trade business without good software is expensive in ways that are easy to overlook. TradesFlow tackles three of the biggest ones:

**1. Lost customer information.** Most trade businesses keep customer details in spreadsheets, notebooks, or just in their head. When a customer calls back, the technician often can't remember what work was done last time, what equipment they have, or what was quoted. That makes the business look disorganized and can lose jobs.

**2. Scheduling headaches.** Without a shared calendar, it's easy to double-book, miss appointments, or waste hours playing phone tag to confirm times. The average trade business loses over $20,000 a year to scheduling problems alone.

**3. Forgotten follow-ups and unpaid invoices.** Studies show that 40% of service quotes never turn into paid work — often because nobody followed up. And when invoices get forgotten, money goes uncollected. TradesFlow automates both.

## How it works *(Features in development — rolling out 2026)*

TradesFlow is being built as a set of straightforward tools that all work together:

**Customer records.** Every customer's name, phone number, address, and history in one place. Search for anyone instantly — no more digging through files.

**Visual calendar.** See all your appointments for the day, week, or month at a glance. Assign jobs to specific team members and track whether each appointment is confirmed, in progress, or done.

**Professional invoices.** Create and send invoices with a few clicks. Track what's been paid and what's overdue. All your invoices are connected to the job they came from, so nothing gets lost.

**Automatic reminders.** TradesFlow sends text messages and emails to customers before their appointments (24 hours and 48 hours ahead) and after jobs are completed. This dramatically reduces no-shows and helps you get more repeat business.

**Price list.** Store all your services and standard prices in one place. When a customer asks for a quote, you can pull from your list instead of guessing or making up numbers on the spot.

**Team roles.** The business owner sees everything. Office managers can handle day-to-day scheduling and invoicing. Technicians in the field see only their assigned jobs. Everyone has the right level of access.

## The Equipment Passport — what makes TradesFlow different *(In development — expected mid-2026)*

The Equipment Passport is TradesFlow's standout feature, and no major competitor offers anything like it.

Here's the idea: when you service a customer's home or business, you're working on specific equipment — a furnace, a water heater, an electrical panel, a dishwasher. The Equipment Passport creates a permanent digital record for each piece of equipment, including:

- The brand, model, and serial number
- When it was installed
- When the warranty expires
- Photos of the equipment (nameplates, condition, installation)
- A log of every repair, maintenance visit, or inspection ever done on it

**Why this matters:** Imagine a customer calls and says "the AC unit you worked on last summer is acting up again." Instead of searching through old paperwork or relying on memory, you pull up their Equipment Passport and immediately see the unit's full history — the brand, model, last repair date, what parts were used, and any patterns in the problems. You look like an expert, and you save time on every return visit.

It also lets you proactively reach out to customers when warranties are about to expire or when annual maintenance is due — creating opportunities for more work and building long-term trust.
