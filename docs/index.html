Operating flow

From first call to paid invoice, one record keeps moving.

The product is designed around the practical path a small field-service team repeats every day.

Capture the caller

Create the customer, service address, notes, and known equipment before the request becomes another loose paper trail.

Schedule the work

Assign the appointment, status, technician, duration, and customer reminders from the same operating record.

Bill cleanly

Turn completed work into invoices, track paid and overdue status, and keep the admin handoff visible.

Signature module

Equipment Passport makes service history visible.

Repeat service should not depend on memory, texts, or old invoices. The asset record stays close to the work.

Customer record

Connect contact details, service addresses, appointments, invoices, equipment, and notes.

Scheduling

Keep status, assignment, duration, confirmations, no-shows, and reminders visible.

Billing handoff

Reduce the gap between completed work and money collected.

Clear enough for a serious buyer to trust the path.

TradesFlow separates the live account foundation, active operational build, and later integrations.

In active development

Planned integrations

Payments, online booking, QuickBooks sync, API access, and inventory after the workflow proves itself.
